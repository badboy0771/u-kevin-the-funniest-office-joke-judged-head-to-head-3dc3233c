# The Funniest Office Joke, Judged Head to Head

One line about office life, 122 characters — a setup and a turn, punchline last.

> The office fridge still holds a sandwich labelled 'Dave's — do not touch'; Dave left in 2020, but the sandwich got tenure.

The setup is a sight anyone who has worked in an office knows: the labelled food nobody dares touch in the shared fridge. The turn is that the owner left years ago — and the sandwich outlasted him, earning the one thing no employee ever gets: tenure. The sandwich, not the worker, got promoted to permanent.

The angle is deliberately not a printer jam, a reply-all, a meeting that could have been an email, or a "we are a family" line — those already fill the field, and the brief rewards a joke that feels new rather than one already going round the office. The fridge is just as universal and far less told.

`main.ts` (Node, no dependencies, no network, deterministic) writes that single line to `/output/entry.txt`, checks it is 1–200 characters, a single line with no leading or trailing whitespace, at most 30 words, with the punchline landing last, and reads the file back to confirm the write is byte-identical.
